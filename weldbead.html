Model Predictive Control for Parameter Optimization

Digital Twin Genetic Algorithm SciPy
~/Weld_Optimizer/

About: Architected a Model Predictive Control (MPC) system to optimize weld parameters for real-world shop floors. Designed a Physics-Informed Digital Twin (utilizing the Rosenthal Heat Source equation) combined with a Transfer Learning ML framework. By synthesizing a 500-sample industrial dataset and adding active sensor inputs for Contact Tip to Work Distance (CTWD) and Plate Interpass Temperature, the model predicts multi-pass thermal dynamics with 98% accuracy. Deployed a Genetic Algorithm optimizer with an Efficiency Penalty to navigate the ill-posed inverse problem, recommending parameter combinations that minimize energy consumption while perfectly hitting geometry targets.

Purpose: To transition from a theoretical lab experiment to a fully industrial Digital Twin that actively compensates for dynamic shop floor conditions (hot plates, long stick-outs) without human trial-and-error.
